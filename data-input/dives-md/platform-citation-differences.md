# Platform Citation Differences: How Each LLM Selects and Cites Sources

## Key Insight
Optimizing for one AI platform risks invisibility on others. Only 11% of cited domains appear across multiple platforms, and brand recommendations disagree 62% of the time (SparkToro/Gumshoe study, 2,961 queries). Each platform has a different retrieval backend: ChatGPT uses Bing (87% citation overlap with Bing top 10), Claude uses Brave Search (86.7% citation overlap), Perplexity uses a proprietary index + Bing hybrid (200B+ URLs), and Google AI Overviews uses FastSearch/RankEmbed (no NavBoost). Source preferences diverge sharply: ChatGPT favors Wikipedia and encyclopedic content, Perplexity favors Reddit and community discussion, Google AI Overviews favors YouTube and user-generated platforms, and Claude favors news/publishers with balanced, non-promotional tone. The frequency a brand appears matters more than position: less than 1 in 1,000 chance of identical recommendation ordering across queries on any single platform.

### Retrieval Architecture by Platform (leak)
- ChatGPT: Grounded via Bing Search index. 87% of citations match Bing's top 10 organic results (Seer Interactive). Evaluates pages by domain authority (~40%), content quality (~35%), platform trust (~25%). Returns 3-6 clickable citations per response. 79% of responses draw from training data (no web search). Web search triggered for only 21% of prompts. 42,592 unique domains cited. TLD distribution: .com 80.41%, .org 11.29%, .uk 2.16%
- Claude: Grounded via Brave Search. 86.7% citation overlap with Brave's top results (Profound, 2025). Shaped by Constitutional AI framework (helpful, harmless, honest). Entity verification weight 30%, technical accuracy 25%, traditional database influence 20%, constitutional alignment 15%. Marketing copy gets 0.8x citation rate (penalized). Risk/limitation sections get 1.7x boost. 31,244 unique domains cited. 5.67 citations per response
- Perplexity: Proprietary index + Bing hybrid. Custom pplx-embed models (0.6B and 4B params, Qwen3 architecture). Six-stage RAG pipeline: query parsing, BM25 + dense retrieval, three-layer ML reranking (L1-L3), structured prompt assembly with pre-embedded citations, constrained LLM synthesis. ~0.7 quality threshold, ~30% of candidates survive. 37,399 unique domains cited. 21.87 citations per response. Retrieves 60+ sources per query, hundreds in Deep Research mode
- Google AI Overviews: FastSearch with RankEmbed signals (no NavBoost click data). Retrieves fewer documents faster than traditional Google Search, prioritizes semantic relevance over backlinks/domain authority. Query fan-out: single query triggers 5-10+ sub-queries, retrieves top 3 organic results per sub-query. 47% of citations from pages below position #5 in organic. Only 17% of cited sources also rank in organic top 10. Average 10.2 links from 4 unique domains per response
- Bing Copilot: Multi-layer architecture with Bing grounding. Content must first appear in Bing results, then win AI selection. Favors structured content: lists, definitions, tables. Extreme citation concentration: top 4 pages account for 90% of citations in tracked datasets. 34% of Bing queries now generate AI answers. Query fanout of 3-5 variations per user question
- Google Gemini: Google's proprietary index + Knowledge Graph. 52.15% of citations from brand-owned websites (highest among platforms). Incorporates Google Business Profile data. Favors structured, factual content with schema markup. 8.34 citations per response

### Source Type Preferences by Platform (leak)
- ChatGPT source hierarchy: Wikipedia 47.9% of top-10 citations, Reddit 11.3%, Forbes 6.8%. Domain authority is primary filter. 48.73% of citations from third-party directories (Yelp, TripAdvisor, MapQuest) for local/subjective queries. After September 2025 algorithm adjustment: Reddit dropped from ~60% to ~10%, Wikipedia from ~55% to <20%. Post-adjustment top sources: Reddit, Wikipedia, Medium, Forbes, LinkedIn
- Perplexity source hierarchy: Reddit 46.7% of top-10 citations, YouTube 13.9%, Gartner 7.0%. Zero Wikipedia citations (0%) per Qwairy Q3 2025 study. Niche industry sources make up 24% of citations for subjective queries. Healthcare: Zocdoc drives citations. Hospitality: TripAdvisor. Sources with JSON-LD schema: 47% top-3 citation rate vs 28% without. 82% citation rate for content under 30 days old, drops to 37% for older content
- Google AI Overviews source hierarchy: Reddit 21.0%, YouTube 18.8%, Quora 14.3%, LinkedIn 13.0% of top-10 citations. More balanced distribution than other platforms. 93.67% cite at least one top-10 organic result. Only 4.5% directly match position #1. YouTube mention is strongest visibility correlator. 85% of citations from content published within last 2 years
- Claude source hierarchy: News/publishers 51% (highest among platforms), topical authority 24%, academic/research 16%, government/institutional 9%. Prioritizes balanced comparison content (1.5x boost), technical documentation (1.4x boost). Penalizes salesy/brand-forward content. Cross-references at least 3 external sources before surfacing claims
- Bing Copilot source hierarchy: Wikipedia cited ~35%. Structured comparison content performs best. Rapid citation decay indicates freshness is critical. IndexNow protocol enables near-instant indexing. Content blocks (self-contained answer units) are the primary citation signal
- Cross-platform Reddit dominance: Reddit = 40.1% of all LLM references across 150,000 citations analyzed (Storyboard18). Reddit traffic grew to 1.4B monthly visits by April 2025. AI citation of Reddit grew 450% from March to June 2025. Posts with <20 upvotes regularly cited. ~80 words, Q&A format, specific tool mentions

### Brand Recommendation Behavior by Platform (leak)
- ChatGPT brand behavior: Names brands in ~36% of relevant queries. Cites specific companies at ~4.5% rate. Averages 2.37 brand mentions per response. Large/dominant brands perform better on ChatGPT (e.g., leading fintech: ~60% visibility). For subjective queries ('best...'), directory sources spike to 46.3%
- Claude brand behavior: Names brands in ~46% of relevant queries (10pp higher than ChatGPT). Cites specific companies at ~9% (2x ChatGPT). Mid-market B2B brands see 4-10x higher visibility vs ChatGPT (cybersecurity platform: 18% vs 4%, employer-of-record: 5x, document management: 10x). Consistently hedges with phrases like 'options include.' Constitutional AI penalizes promotional content
- Perplexity brand behavior: Cites 2-6 sources per answer with 92% citation integration rate. Higher competitor citation rate (~18%) than ChatGPT or Claude. Discovers new content within 2-3 days. Citations decay to 0.5% after 1-2 months. Conversion rate: 10.5% (6x Google organic). 60-70M daily queries processed
- Google AI Overviews brand behavior: Averages 6.02 brand mentions per response (highest). 47% of citations from pages ranking below position #5. Traditional organic ranking correlation dropped to r=0.18 (from r=0.43 pre-2024). YouTube mentions strongest visibility correlator. Semantic completeness = #1 ranking factor (r=0.87): content scoring 8.5/10+ is 4.2x more likely to be selected
- Cross-platform disagreement: Brand mentions disagree 62% of the time across platforms (SparkToro/Gumshoe, 2,961 queries, 600 volunteers). Less than 1% chance of identical brand list across 100 prompts. Less than 1 in 1,000 chance of same list in same order. Average brand recommendation: 28% genuine endorsement, 41% neutral, 19% cautious, 12% hallucination. Visibility percentage (how often you appear) is the stable metric, not position
- Gemini brand behavior: 52.15% of citations from brand-owned websites (highest platform). Favors structured, factual content from brand domains. Google Business Profile data influences but isn't directly cited. Schema markup and local landing pages are primary drivers

### Cross-Platform Citation Optimization (exploit)
- Multi-platform minimum strategy: Only 11% of domains cited across both ChatGPT and Perplexity. Must optimize for each platform's retrieval backend independently. Sites appearing on 4+ platforms are 2.8x more likely to show up in ChatGPT responses. Brand search volume is the #1 predictor of LLM citations (r=0.334), outweighing backlinks
- Content optimization with measured impact: Adding citations to content: +115.1% visibility for rank #5 sites. Quotations: +37%. Statistics: +22%. Comparative listicles: 32.5% of all AI citations (highest format). Content updated within 30 days gets 3.2x more citations across all platforms. 65% of AI bot traffic targets content from past year
- Platform-specific tactics: For ChatGPT: get Wikipedia presence, optimize for Bing rankings, use encyclopedic tone. For Claude: write balanced comparisons, include limitations/risks, avoid promotional language. For Perplexity: add JSON-LD schema, keep content fresh (<30 days), target Reddit with authentic answers. For Google AI Overviews: optimize for semantic completeness, use clear heading hierarchies, don't rely on organic rank alone
- Monitoring tools: Profound (AI-native, $20M funding, enterprise LLM tracking), Semrush AI Visibility Index (ChatGPT + Google AI Mode, 130-page benchmark report), Otterly AI (5,000+ users, Semrush App Center integration, GEO workflows), Gumshoe.ai (visibility frequency tracking), Peec AI, Sight AI, xSeek. Track reference rates, not rankings
- Perplexity publisher revenue model: Publishers' Program launched July 2024 (TIME, Fortune, Der Spiegel). Comet Plus: $42.5M pool, 80/20 revenue split favoring publishers. Ads tested Nov 2024 ($50+ CPM), generated $20K in 2024 of $34M total revenue. Ads paused Oct 2025, fully abandoned Feb 2026. User trust prioritized over ad revenue. Not a content licensing deal (no LLM training on publisher content)

### Calculations
- Citation Volume Index: Average citations per response varies dramatically by platform, reflecting different retrieval philosophies. Perplexity's 21.87 citations reflect its 60+ source retrieval and three-layer reranking. ChatGPT's 7.92 reflects selective Bing grounding. Claude's 5.67 reflects cautious, verified sourcing. Google AI Mode's 8.34 reflects FastSearch's abbreviated result sets. Affects: Total citation volume determines how many source slots are available per response. Higher volume = more opportunities for content to be cited.
- Source Preference Divergence Matrix: Each platform's top-10 citation sources reveal fundamentally different retrieval philosophies. ChatGPT anchors on Wikipedia (encyclopedic authority), Perplexity on Reddit (community consensus), Google AI Overviews on a balanced mix (YouTube, Reddit, Quora). Only 11% of domains appear across multiple platforms, meaning 89% of the citation landscape is platform-specific. Affects: Content must match each platform's preferred source type. Wikipedia optimization helps ChatGPT but not Perplexity. Reddit presence helps Perplexity and Google AI Overviews but less ChatGPT.
- Brand Recommendation Consistency Score: AI brand recommendation lists are extremely volatile. Less than 1% chance of identical brand lists across 100 identical prompts on any single platform. Less than 1 in 1,000 chance of same list in same order. Visibility frequency (how often brand appears across many queries) is the only stable metric. City of Hope appeared in 97% of cancer care responses on ChatGPT but ranked first in only 25 of 69 mentions. Affects: Position in AI recommendation lists is meaningless as a KPI. Frequency of appearance (visibility rate) is the stable, trackable metric.
- Claude vs ChatGPT Brand Visibility Gap: Claude names brands more often (46% vs 36%) and cites owned domains at 2x the rate of ChatGPT (9% vs 4.5%). Mid-market B2B brands see 4-10x higher visibility on Claude due to Constitutional AI rewarding balanced, non-promotional content. Large dominant brands may actually perform better on ChatGPT due to stronger training data frequency effects. Affects: Platform choice determines which brands gain visibility. Mid-market B2B brands should prioritize Claude optimization. Enterprise brands naturally dominate ChatGPT via training data frequency.
- Freshness Decay Curve: Content freshness dramatically affects citation probability, but sensitivity varies by platform. Perplexity is most freshness-sensitive (82% citation rate for <30-day content, 37% for older). ChatGPT is moderately sensitive (76.4% within 60 days). Google AI Overviews is least sensitive (61% within 90 days). Perplexity citations decay to 0.5% after 1-2 months. Affects: Content refresh cadence must match platform targeting. Perplexity requires near-monthly updates. ChatGPT tolerates bi-monthly. Google AI Overviews tolerates quarterly.
- Conversion Rate by Platform: AI-referred traffic converts at dramatically higher rates than traditional search because users arrive with higher intent and pre-qualified understanding. ChatGPT referrals convert at 15.9% (9x Google organic baseline of 1.76%). Perplexity converts at 10.5% (6x). Perplexity separately measured at 14.2% vs Google's 2.8% (5x multiplier). Affects: Even though AI search reduces total clicks (93% zero-click in Google AI Mode), each click that does occur is 5-9x more valuable. Quality over quantity in the AI citation era.

### Cross-Platform Citation Pipeline Comparison
- User Query: User asks a question. Query is tokenized and embedded. Each platform processes differently: ChatGPT may not trigger web search (79% parametric), Perplexity always retrieves, Google AI Overviews fan out to 5-10+ sub-queries, Claude selectively searches via Brave.
- Retrieval Backend: ChatGPT: Bing index (87% overlap with Bing top 10). Claude: Brave Search (86.7% overlap). Perplexity: proprietary index + Bing hybrid (200B+ URLs, BM25 + dense embeddings). Google AI Overviews: FastSearch/RankEmbed (no NavBoost, semantic over authority). Bing Copilot: Bing index with multi-layer architecture.
- Candidate Ranking: ChatGPT: domain authority 40%, content quality 35%, platform trust 25%. Perplexity: three-layer ML reranking (L1-L3), ~0.7 quality threshold, ~30% survival rate. Google AI Overviews: semantic completeness r=0.87 (#1 factor), traditional rank correlation dropped to r=0.18. Claude: entity verification 30%, technical accuracy 25%, constitutional alignment 15%.
- Source Selection: Platforms select from ranked candidates. ChatGPT: 3-6 citations, Wikipedia-heavy. Perplexity: 21.87 avg citations, Reddit-heavy, pre-embedded in structured prompt before LLM generation. Google AI Overviews: 10.2 links from 4 unique domains, YouTube + Reddit. Claude: 5.67 citations, news/publishers, penalizes promotional content.
- Response Generation: LLM generates response. Key difference: Perplexity embeds citation markers in the prompt BEFORE generation (architecturally bound). ChatGPT appends citations after generation. Google AI Overviews synthesize across sub-query results. Claude cross-references 3+ sources before surfacing claims.
- Citation Display: Perplexity: inline numbered citations with source sidebar. ChatGPT: clickable source links, footer format. Claude: inline links in context. Google AI Overviews: expandable source chips. Bing Copilot: superscript numbered citations with source panel. Different display formats affect user click behavior.

### Contradictions
- Public (GEO/LLMO industry (unified optimization approach)): Optimize your content once for AI search and you'll appear across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. The same content optimization techniques work everywhere. vs Internal (Cross-platform citation data: <a href="https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/">11% domain overlap, 62% brand disagreement</a>, platform-specific retrieval backends): Only 11% of cited domains appear across multiple platforms. Brand recommendations disagree 62% of the time. Each platform has a different retrieval backend (Bing, Brave, proprietary index, FastSearch), different source preferences (Wikipedia vs Reddit vs YouTube), and different ranking signals (domain authority vs semantic completeness vs constitutional alignment). ChatGPT's September 2025 source shake-up was isolated to ChatGPT only. Optimizing for one platform may have zero effect on another.
- Public (Traditional SEO (authority = ranking)): Domain authority and backlinks determine where you rank. Build links, improve authority, get cited. vs Internal (Platform-specific ranking signals diverge from traditional SEO): Google AI Overviews: traditional organic ranking correlation dropped to r=0.18 (from r=0.43 pre-2024). 47% of citations from pages below position #5. Perplexity: 92.78% of cited pages have fewer than 10 referring domains. Brand search volume (r=0.334) is a stronger predictor than backlinks ('weak or neutral' correlation). Semantic completeness (r=0.87) is the #1 factor for AI Overviews. Claude penalizes promotional content regardless of domain authority.
- Public (Perplexity (trust-first positioning)): Perplexity provides accurate, well-cited answers you can trust. Every answer includes inline citations to verified sources. vs Internal (Columbia Journalism Review audit + independent accuracy tests): Columbia Journalism Review found a 37% error rate in Perplexity answers. Two failure modes: misattribution (correct info, wrong source) and fabrication (wrong info, irrelevant citation). Community reports of 0 out of 6 correct citations in some cases. System is vulnerable to SEO-optimized content that signals relevance without accuracy. Despite 21.87 citations per response (highest among platforms), citation volume does not equal citation accuracy.
- Public (AI search platforms (fair, algorithmic source selection)): AI search selects the best, most relevant sources algorithmically. The cream rises to the top. vs Internal (Extreme citation concentration + retrieval backend dependency): Bing Copilot: top 4 pages account for 90% of all citations. ChatGPT: 87% of citations match Bing's top 10 (inheriting Bing's biases). Claude: 86.7% overlap with Brave Search results. Google AI Overviews: FastSearch retrieves 'fewer documents' with 'lower quality than Search's fully ranked web results' (court filings). Each platform's citation is largely a function of which search backend it uses, not independent quality assessment.

### Overview
Six major AI platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot) use fundamentally different citation architectures, source preferences, and brand recommendation behaviors. Perplexity averages 21.87 citations per response vs ChatGPT's 7.92. Only 11% of cited domains overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity. Brand recommendations disagree 62% of the time across platforms (SparkToro/Gumshoe, 2,961 queries). ChatGPT relies on Wikipedia (47.9% of top citations), Perplexity relies on Reddit (46.7%), and Google AI Overviews balances user-generated and professional content. Claude names brands in 46% of relevant queries vs ChatGPT's 36%, with mid-market B2B brands seeing 4-10x higher visibility on Claude. Each platform's retrieval backend (Bing for ChatGPT, Brave for Claude, proprietary index for Perplexity, FastSearch/RankEmbed for Google AI Overviews) creates distinct optimization requirements.
