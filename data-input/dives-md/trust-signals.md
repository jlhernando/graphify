# Trust Signals Across AI Platforms: How Each LLM Decides What to Cite

## Key Insight
AI platforms use four distinct trust architectures: (1) Google AI Overviews: E-E-A-T with entity-level Knowledge Graph verification (semantic completeness r=0.87, factual verification r=0.89). (2) ChatGPT: Bing rank passthrough with domain/brand authority as #1 factor; sites with 32K+ referring domains are 3.5x more likely to be cited. (3) Claude: Brave Search + Constitutional AI filtering; single factual errors can disqualify a source. (4) Perplexity: 3-layer ML reranker with ~0.7 quality threshold that drops entire result sets rather than serve low-quality citations. Traditional domain authority (DA) has collapsed to r=0.18 correlation. Entity recognition, cross-platform presence, and community validation (Reddit, Wikipedia) now drive AI trust.

### Platform-Specific Trust Architectures (patent)
- Google AI Overviews: E-E-A-T + Knowledge Graph. r=0.81 correlation between E-E-A-T signals and AI Overview selection. 96% of content comes from sources with verified E-E-A-T. Semantic completeness (r=0.87) and factual verification (r=0.89) outweigh traditional SEO signals. 47% of citations come from pages ranking below position #5 in organic results, showing AI selection is decoupled from traditional rankings. Domain Authority correlation dropped to r=0.18
- ChatGPT: Bing passthrough + brand authority. Sends queries to Bing's API, fetches full content, synthesizes answers. 87% of citations match Bing's top 10. 60% of queries answered from parametric knowledge alone (no web retrieval). Domain + brand authority is #1 factor. Sites with 32K+ referring domains are 3.5x more likely to be cited. Trustworthiness weighted 10/10. 3.2x more brand mentions than linked citations
- Claude: Brave Search + Constitutional AI. Uses Brave Search (86.7% citation overlap). More conservative than ChatGPT: refuses citation when claims lack support. Single factual errors can disqualify a source. Prefers 3-4 independent authoritative domains. Constitutional AI causes caution in YMYL categories. Preference for content that separates facts from judgment. Recommended update cycle: every 2-4 weeks
- Perplexity: 3-layer ML reranker. 6-stage pipeline retrieving 60+ sources per query. L3 XGBoost reranker with ~0.7 quality threshold drops entire result sets rather than serve low-quality citations. Citations pre-embedded in prompts before LLM synthesis, not retrofitted. Company websites systematically discounted as inherently promotional. 59 potential ranking signals identified including topic classification
- Gemini: Knowledge Graph-first. Maps queries to known entities in Google's Knowledge Graph before retrieving. Evaluates content across entity connections, formats, and authoritative references. Prioritizes content tracing claims to academic papers, patents, and official documentation over secondary blog posts. Considers cross-platform brand presence. Answer-ready blocks of 40-80 words prioritized

### Universal Trust Signals (leak)
- Wikipedia: universal trust anchor. Top 10 most-cited domain on every AI platform (4.3M citations). ChatGPT: 47.9%, AI Overviews: 18%, Perplexity: 12.5%. Comprises ~22% of major LLM training data. Wikidata provides structured, machine-readable data for entity verification. Brands without Wikidata entities are invisible to AI's factual grounding layer
- Reddit: community-validated authority. #1 most-cited domain overall (7.3M citations across 7 platforms). 40.1% citation frequency across 150K analyzed citations. Citations from Reddit in AI overviews grew 450% from March to June 2025. Monopolizes 'alternatives' queries. 85% of consumers find UGC more trustworthy than brand content, and LLMs mirror this
- Entity recognition as trust signal. Google maintains 54 billion entities in its Knowledge Graph. Pages with 15+ connected entities show 4.8x higher AI Overview selection probability. Top 3 brands in any category hold 70% of LLM mentions (Gartner). Brands lacking structured 'triples' (subject-predicate-object) remain unresolvable. Entity disambiguation schema (SameAs, knowsAbout pointing to Wikidata, LinkedIn, Crunchbase) is highest-leverage implementation
- Author-level signals. Bylined content with industry credentials gains significantly more AI visibility. Perplexity's L3 reranker evaluates named authors and institutional credibility. Claude specifically recognizes credentialed authors in regulated fields. About pages function as trust anchors: credentials, awards, certifications, years in business. However, author signal is weaker than domain/brand signals for most platforms
- Source consensus. Leading platforms now reference 2-4 independent domains for consensus before surfacing data. Claims appearing on only one source face active scrutiny. However, 50-90% of LLM citations don't fully support attached claims (Nature Communications). Knowledge Graphs serve as structured verification layers that mitigate hallucinations

### New Authority Metrics (Replacing Domain Authority) (doj)
- Domain Authority collapse. DA correlation with AI citations: r=0.18 (down from r=0.43 pre-2024). Traditional backlink-based authority has been displaced by brand signals, entity density, and community validation. 80% of LLM citations don't rank in Google's top 100 for the same query
- Brand search volume is #1 predictor. 0.334 correlation (Digital Bloom 2025), outweighing backlinks. Brand mentions correlate 3x more strongly with AI visibility than backlinks. Brands in top 25% for web mentions earn 10x more AI citations
- Cross-platform presence. Presence across 4+ platforms yields 2.8x higher ChatGPT citation probability. Only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity, making multi-platform presence essential
- Schema and entity linking. FAQPage schema: 41% citation rate vs 15% without. JSON-LD in Perplexity: 47% vs 28% Top-3 rate. ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini all actively process schema markup (SearchVIU, Oct 2025). Only 4% of schema-present pages implement entity-linking. Generic schema shows 18pp citation penalty vs entity-linked schema
- Freshness as trust signal. Platforms differ: Perplexity most aggressive (50% of citations from current year, peak at 2-3 days post-publish). ChatGPT: 76.4% of cited pages updated within 30 days. AI Overviews: moderate (85% from last 2 years). Claude: balanced (2-4 week update cycle)

### Optimization by Trust Architecture (exploit)
- For Google AI Overviews: Maximize E-E-A-T signals. Author pages with credentials. Cite peer-reviewed sources and Tier-1 publications. Build Knowledge Graph entity density (15+ connected entities). 40-80 word answer-ready blocks. Entity disambiguation schema pointing to Wikidata
- For ChatGPT: Optimize for Bing first. 87% of citations match Bing top 10. Build referring domain count (32K+ = 3.5x citation rate). Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools. Allow OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt. Update content every 30 days (3.2x boost)
- For Claude: Prioritize factual accuracy (single errors disqualify). Cite 3-4 independent authoritative domains. Separate facts from judgment. Avoid promotional language and absolutist claims. Add visible freshness timestamps. Optimize for Brave Search ranking
- For Perplexity: Earn coverage from Tier-1 publications (structurally favored over brand content). JSON-LD schema (47% vs 28% citation rate). Front-load answers in first 100 words (BLUF). Update content every 2-3 days for peak citation window. Reddit presence essential (46.7% of Perplexity citations)
- For Gemini: Build entity connections in Google's Knowledge Graph. Cross-platform brand presence (Reddit, G2, LinkedIn, YouTube). Trace claims to academic papers and official documentation. 40-80 word extractable blocks. Brand-owned websites accepted (52.15% citation rate)
- Universal: Wikipedia + Wikidata presence. Reddit community validation. Cross-platform brand mentions (top 25% = 10x citations). Entity-linked schema. Named, credentialed authors. Regular content freshness updates

### Contradictions
- Public (SEO industry): E-E-A-T is the universal standard for AI search quality across all platforms. vs Internal (Platform architecture analysis): E-E-A-T is Google-specific (r=0.81 for AI Overviews). ChatGPT uses Bing's authority signals. Claude uses Brave Search + Constitutional AI. Perplexity uses a proprietary ML reranker. Each platform evaluates trust differently.
- Public (SEO tools): Domain Authority (DA) predicts AI citation performance. vs Internal (<a href="https://wellows.com/blog/google-ai-overviews-ranking-factors/">Wellows correlation study</a>): DA correlation with AI citations has dropped to r=0.18 (from r=0.43). Brand search volume (0.334) and cross-platform mentions (3x more predictive than backlinks) have replaced DA as the primary authority signals.
- Public (Brand marketers): Building our own website's authority is the path to AI visibility. vs Internal (<a href="https://authoritytech.io/blog/how-perplexity-selects-sources-algorithm-2026">AuthorityTech Perplexity analysis</a>): Perplexity systematically discounts company websites as inherently promotional. Third-party mentions are 3x more correlated with AI visibility than brand-owned content. The exception: Gemini cites brand-owned websites at 52.15%.
- Public (Traditional search): Ranking #1 on Google means you'll be cited in AI search too. vs Internal (<a href="https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/2025-ai-citation-llm-visibility-report/">Digital Bloom 2025</a>): 80% of LLM citations don't rank in Google's top 100 for the same query. Only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity. AI trust is platform-specific, not search-ranking-derived.

### Overview
Google's E-E-A-T framework does not apply universally. Each AI platform evaluates source trust differently: Google AI Overviews use E-E-A-T with r=0.81 correlation to selection. ChatGPT inherits trust from Bing's ranking (87% citation overlap). Claude uses Brave Search (86.7% overlap) filtered through Constitutional AI principles. Perplexity runs a proprietary 3-layer ML reranker that systematically discounts brand-owned content. Traditional domain authority has collapsed (r=0.18 correlation with AI citations, down from r=0.43). The new authority metrics: brand search volume (0.334 correlation), Knowledge Graph entity density (15+ entities = 4.8x selection), and cross-platform presence (Reddit #1 at 7.3M citations, Wikipedia #2 at 4.3M).
